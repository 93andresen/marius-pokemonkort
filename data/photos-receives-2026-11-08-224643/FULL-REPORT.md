## 6. PokeWallet API — cannot do this

Grepping all of `docs/` for `sealed|booster|elite trainer|product type|display box`
returns **0 results**. Every endpoint is card-centric:

| Endpoint | Takes | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `/search` | card name, set code, card number, `set_id + number` | cards with prices |
| `/prices/:setCode` | set code or group_id — **PRO only** | "prices for every **card** in a set" |
| `/cards/:id` | card id | one card |
| `/sets`, `/sets/:code` | set code | set metadata |
| `/images/:id` | card id | artwork |

There is no sealed-product entity and no product-type parameter. Japanese sets
exist but come back with a negative `set_id` and `cardmarket: null`, so even the
singles are TCGPlayer-USD only.

**No PokeWallet calls were made during this work.** `logs/ratelog.csv` is
untouched (newest entry 2026-10-10-140910). The 100/hour, 1000/day budget is
intact.

## 7. Is any of this worth it?

### 7.1 What the data settles

- The offer is **wholesale-priced**. A consistent 1,02×–2,58× retail markup
  across 16 independent sets is what a real distributor price looks like. It is
  not a scam, and it is not a fire sale.
- The offer is **not a bargain**. Nothing is mispriced in your favour.

### 7.2 Why the spread is smaller than it looks

**The 1,43× is a retail markup, not profit.** It has to cover FINN fees,
shipping, VAT, and months of your time.

**The realistic gross spread is ≈ 1,1M NOK, not 4M.** Median FINN ask across the
16 matched sets is ≈ 1 275 NOK/box. Selling all 3 480 boxes at that:

```
3 480 × 1 275  =  4 437 000 NOK
cost           =  3 333 065 NOK
gross spread   =  1 103 935 NOK
```

That is before fees, shipping, VAT, dead stock, and the fact that you cannot
actually sell 3 480 boxes.

**Liquidity is the binding constraint.** FINN's entire Japanese sealed supply is
31 listings, almost all single boxes. The offer is 50–500 boxes per set. The
market cannot absorb the volume at these prices — you would realistically move a
few dozen and sit on the rest.

**21 of 37 sets are unvalidated**, including the two largest lines (Eevee Heroes
and 25th Anniversary Collection, ≈270 000 NOK combined).

### 7.3 Verdict

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Are the ¥ prices fair? | Yes — consistent with wholesale. |
| Is this a good deal? | No. It is a normal wholesale price. |
| Can FINN validate it? | Partly — 16 of 37 sets, median 1,43×. |
| Is the margin real? | ≈1,1M NOK gross on 3,33M NOK cost, before costs and liquidity. |
| Is it worth scraping more? | Only the bulk lot, and the 31 boxes for a clean table. |

## 8. What to scrape, if anything

| Target | Worth it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `479522079` — 37 500 NOK bulk lot | **Yes, first** | The only wholesale-scale listing. Its description may reveal a real per-box wholesale price achieved in Norway — the one number that could change the conclusion. |
| The 31 single-box ads | **Yes, low value** | Gives a properly sourced per-set table instead of "lowest ask". ~31 requests. Will not change the 1,43× picture. |
| The `480586xxx` / `480587xxx` cluster | **Marginal** | ~18 ads from one reseller. A price list, but one seller's opinion, not a market. |
| Singles, ETBs, English/Korean/Chinese boxes | **No** | Different product. The price is the acrylic case or the language, not the box. |

**Bottom line:** scrape the bulk lot, and the 31 boxes if you want a clean table.
Do not expect scraping to turn this into a good deal — it will confirm the
wholesale reading, not overturn it. The decision this data supports is whether
you want to run a retail operation on 3 480 boxes, not whether the ¥ prices are
fair.
